Who We Are
Max Pawn Luxury is a specialized pawn shop at 6040 West Sahara Avenue in Las Vegas that focuses on authenticated designer handbags, fine jewelry, watches, and other luxury items from top brands*. The business was established in 2009 and operates conveniently near the Las Vegas Strip.
What We Offer
We buy, sell, and pawn luxury goods including designer brands like Gucci, Rolex, Louis Vuitton, and Hermes*. We offer flexible payment options in the form of layaway and through partnerships with third-party companies. We have built a reputation for making luxury items more accessible and affordable while maintaining quality standards.
*All items are pre-owned; trademarks are owned by their respective brand owners. No brand owner endorses or has any association and/or affiliation with Max Pawn Luxury. Certain items are certified as authentic by a third party. Brands identified in our stores are not involved in the authentication of the products being sold.
