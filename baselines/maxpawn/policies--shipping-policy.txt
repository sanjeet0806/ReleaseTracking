Shipping policy
Processing Time
Your order will be shipped via FedEx within three business days from the purchase date.
Orders placed on weekends, holidays, or outside our regular business hours will be processed and prepared for shipment within the next three business days .
Please note that we currently ship only within the United States and do not offer international shipping.
Delivery Confirmation
Orders totaling $500 or less will be shipped via FedEx Ground with delivery confirmation.
Signature Required
For orders ranging from $501 to $2,999 , shipping will be via FedEx Ground or FedEx Express Saver and will require signature confirmation upon delivery.
Adult Signature Required (21+)
Orders totaling $3,000 or more will be shipped via FedEx Express and will require an Adult Signature (21 years or older) upon delivery .
Important Notice: Discrepancies between billing and shipping information may result in delays. To ensure a smooth delivery process, please provide a valid phone number so we can contact you if address verification is needed.
