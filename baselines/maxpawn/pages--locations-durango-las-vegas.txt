Who We Are
Max Pawn Luxury is located at 6410 South Durango Drive in the southwest valley and is the brand’s third Las Vegas location. We specialize in pawning, buying, and selling authenticated* designer handbags, fine jewelry, watches, and other valuables from top name brands.
What We Offer
This boutique-style location redefines secondhand shopping and offers a large inventory of designer items both in-store and online. The store provides a unique shopping experience with a focus on high-quality luxury items. We offer pawn loans for high-quality, luxury items, layaway with only 10% down and easy monthly payments, and we partner with third-party companies to provide flexible payment plans.
*All items are pre-owned; trademarks are owned by their respective brand owners. No brand owner endorses or has any association and/or affiliation with Max Pawn Luxury. Certain items are certified as authentic by a third party. Brands identified in our stores are not involved in the authentication of the products being sold.
