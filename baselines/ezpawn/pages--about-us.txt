About EZPAWN
Since we were founded in 1974 in Austin, Texas, our goal has remained the same: to make our customers’ lives easier. Today, at more than 500 pawn shops across the U.S., we help everyday people with options to get quick access to cash by pawning or selling their items of value. We also sell quality, brand-name, pre-owned merchandise.
