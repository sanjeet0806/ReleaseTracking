Refund policy
1. Return and Refund Policy
Return Window & Shipping
Jewelry items must be postmarked within 7 days of the delivery date.
The customer is responsible for return shipping including insurance, charged at a flat rate $30 USD
All return shipments are insured.
Eligibility Criteria
Timely Return
Item(s) must be postmarked within 7 days of the delivery date.
Return Tag
The security/return tag must remain attached to the jewelry item at the time of return.
Tag language - "not returnable if removed"
If the security tag has been removed, the item is not eligible for return under any circumstances, and no refund will be issued. There are no exceptions to this policy.
Condition
Item must be returned in the same condition as received, including all original packaging and accessories.
If the item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 48 hours of delivery, via onlineexclusive@ezpawn.com
Returns must be securely packaged. If the item is damaged due to improper return packaging, a deduction may be applied to the refund to cover the loss in value.
Customized or altered jewelry items are not eligible for return.
Refunds
Refunds are issued only after the returned item has been received and inspected.
Once approved, the refund is processed to the original payment method within 10 business days. Banks or card issuers may require additional time to post the refund.
Exchanges
We do not currently offer exchanges on any items.
2. Shipping Policy

All shipments are insured.

3. Cancellation Policy
Orders may be cancelled only before they have shipped or been picked up by the shipper.
Once an order has shipped or been picked up, it cannot be cancelled. There are no exceptions.
